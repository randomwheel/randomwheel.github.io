📜 About the Game

What is 2048?

2048 is a single-player sliding tile puzzle originally created by Gabriele Cirulli in March 2014. It became a viral sensation overnight, captivating millions with its deceptively simple mechanics and deeply strategic depth.

The game is played on a grid (classically 4×4) where numbered tiles slide freely. Each slide spawns a new tile, and identical tiles merge when they collide. The challenge is to keep the board from filling up while working toward the coveted 2048 tile.

Our version adds 3 board sizes (3×3, 4×4, 5×5), an Undo button, persistent Best Score, and beautiful warm-cream tile styling — all free, right in your browser.

💡 Strategy

Pro Tips to Reach 2048

These strategies separate good players from great ones.

01

Corner Strategy

Keep your highest-value tile locked in one corner (e.g. bottom-left) and never let it move away. Build everything toward it.

02

Snake Pattern

Arrange tiles in a descending snake (zigzag) pattern from your corner. This makes merges predictable and controllable.

03

Avoid Upward Slides

If your big tile is in the bottom-left, avoid pressing Up whenever possible — it can displace your anchor tile catastrophically.

04

Keep the Board Sparse

Always aim to have several empty cells. A crowded board removes flexibility and leads to game-over faster than anything else.

05

Merge Small Tiles First

Clearing low-value clutter (2s and 4s) early prevents them from becoming blockers later when the board gets tight.

06

Plan Two Moves Ahead

Before each slide, visualise the next move too. A great slide that sets up an even better follow-up is worth more than an isolated merge.
