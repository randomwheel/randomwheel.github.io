💡 Strategy

Cookie Clicker Tips & Strategy

Maximize your cookies per second with these expert idle clicker tips.

01

Prioritize CPS Over Clicks

Cookies Per Second from buildings scales infinitely. Focus early spending on buildings that give the most CPS per cookie cost.

02

Never Ignore Upgrades

Upgrades often multiply entire building categories. A 2× upgrade to Grandmas is worth more than buying two extra Grandmas.

03

Click Golden Cookies Fast

Golden cookies only appear for 13 seconds. Click Frenzy multiplies your CPS by 7 for 77 seconds — worth thousands of manual clicks.

04

Buy in Bulk Strategically

Each building's price scales as you buy more. Buying 10 at once is often better value — wait until you can batch-purchase.

05

Let It Run Overnight

Cookie Clicker rewards idle players! Leave it running and collect offline cookies. A night's sleep can give hours of production.

06

Balance Click & Idle Power

Late-game, cursor upgrades can make each manual click worth thousands of CPS. Don't stop clicking even with a huge passive income!
