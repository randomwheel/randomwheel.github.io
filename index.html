About Our Free Online Spin Wheel

RandomWheel is the web's most comprehensive free spin wheel application. Whether you need a quick yes-or-no answer, want to pick a random name, organize a raffle, or create an unforgettable party game — we've got you covered.

What Is a Spin Wheel?

A spin wheel (also called a random wheel spinner, wheel of fortune, or decision wheel) randomly selects one item from a list. Enter your choices, click spin, and the wheel lands on a random result. It's the fairest, most entertaining way to make decisions.

Why Use a Random Wheel Spinner?

Random wheel spinners eliminate decision fatigue and add fun and fairness to choices. Teachers use them for engagement, event organizers for raffles, friends for game nights, and professionals for team assignments.
