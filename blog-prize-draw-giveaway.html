02. Step-by-Step: Running Your Prize Draw

Follow these seven steps for a giveaway that feels professional, fair, and genuinely exciting for your audience:

1

Define your entry method

Decide how people enter — comment to win, follow + share, form submission, ticket purchase, or in-person raffle stub. Your entry method determines how you collect the names that go on the wheel.

2

Collect and clean your entries

Export your entrant list and remove duplicates. For social media comments, copy usernames — not full names — to preserve privacy. Paste the final list into a text file, one name per line.

3

Load names into the wheel

Open the spin wheel, click "Edit Wheel," and paste your list. Each line becomes a segment. The wheel auto-resizes — it handles 5 entries or 500 with equal ease.

4

Go live (highly recommended)

Start your stream, share your screen showing the wheel, and confirm to your audience that every entrant is on it. This "show before spin" moment is the most trust-building thing you can do.

5

Spin — and let the drama unfold

Hit spin and let your audience watch. Don't skip or stop the animation. The anticipation is part of the experience. The ticking sound increases excitement — keep the volume up.

6

Confirm the winner publicly

Screenshot the result and post it. Tag the winner on social media. Give them 48–72 hours to claim before drawing again — state this deadline upfront in your giveaway rules.

7

Archive proof of the draw

Save the screen recording of the spin. Many platforms allow you to save your live stream — keep it accessible for 30 days post-draw in case of disputes. Transparency doesn't end at the spin.

03. Platform-by-Platform Giveaway Tips

Each platform has its own giveaway culture and best practices. Here's how to optimise your spin wheel draw for each:

Instagram & TikTok

Run the draw live using Instagram Live or TikTok Live. Announce the spin 24 hours before going live to maximise attendance. Pin a comment with the winner's tag immediately after the spin so it's permanently visible to anyone who watches the replay.

YouTube

YouTube giveaways perform best as mid-video reveals. Build suspense through the video's first half, then share your screen with the wheel loaded. The "show the wheel before spinning" moment creates a natural dramatic pause that drives watch time.

Twitch & Live Streams

Twitch audiences are particularly sceptical of rigged draws. Use the wheel's "one spin removes the name" feature so viewers can watch the entrant pool shrink in real time for multi-prize draws. This builds enormous trust with gaming and streaming communities.

In-Person Events

Project the wheel on a screen at your venue. Collect names via paper slips, QR code form, or ticket number. Enter them live on stage. The collective excitement of a room watching the wheel spin together is genuinely hard to replicate with any other method.

05. Legal Basics Every Giveaway Needs

Before you spin, make sure you've covered these essential legal bases. Requirements vary by country, but these apply almost universally:

✅ Giveaway Compliance Checklist

  • ✓No purchase necessary: In most jurisdictions, requiring payment to enter a prize draw makes it a lottery requiring a licence. Keep entry free.
  • ✓State the odds: If you have 200 entrants and one prize, make this clear. "1 winner from all eligible entries" is sufficient.
  • ✓Publish clear rules: Entry deadline, prize description, eligibility (age/region), and how the winner will be notified must all be stated publicly.
  • ✓Claim deadline: State how long winners have to respond (48–72 hours is standard) and what happens if they don't (a redraw).
  • ✓Platform compliance: Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok each have specific promotion policies. Ensure your giveaway format doesn't violate their terms.
  • ✓Data handling: If you collect emails or personal details, state how that data will be used. GDPR (EU) and similar laws require this explicitly.
⚠️ Important

This article provides general guidance only. For giveaways with high-value prizes or large audiences, consult a legal professional familiar with consumer promotion law in your region.

06. Pro Tips for Maximum Giveaway Impact

  • Weight entries by action. Add a name twice for entrants who both follow and share — the wheel naturally reflects weighted probability.
  • Announce the draw date well in advance. A 48-hour countdown post before the live draw dramatically increases attendance and last-minute entries.
  • Run multiple small prizes over one big one. Three winners of smaller prizes generates more excitement than one jackpot winner — more people stay engaged until the end.
  • Use the "remove after spin" feature. For multi-prize draws, removing each winner from the wheel before spinning again shows everyone their odds increasing — highly engaging.
  • Record and repost the moment. The clip of the wheel stopping on the winner's name is highly shareable. Post it as a Reel, Short, or TikTok immediately after the draw.
